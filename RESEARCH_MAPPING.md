# 🔬 Academic & Industry Research Mapping

This document maps **ResilientIndia AI** against current academic research and identifies how the platform solves critical gaps in contemporary supply chain systems.

## 1. Real-Time AI Simulation vs. Legacy Static Planning
* **Current Gap:** Traditional supply chain risk management relies on static contingency planning and historical data, making it inadequate for dynamic geopolitical crises (IJSFT).
* **ResilientIndia AI Solution:** Implements a Gemini RAG vector engine and multi-agent AI system that ingests unstructured threat feeds and executes real-time digital twin simulations in <3 seconds.
* **Citation:** *Geopolitical Risk Simulation Using AI-Powered Digital Twin Systems in Global Logistics* ([Link](https://ijsrm.net/index.php/ijsrm/article/view/7265))

## 2. Maritime Chokepoint Rerouting & Latency Reduction
* **Current Gap:** Disruption in global chokepoints (Strait of Hormuz, Bab-el-Mandeb) forces Cape of Good Hope rerouting, adding 10–14 days of delay and tripling freight rates while organizations spend weeks manually re-planning logistics (UNCTAD / IRE Journals).
* **ResilientIndia AI Solution:** Features an Adaptive Logistics Architect Agent that automatically calculates alternate maritime corridors, delay impact, and Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) drawdowns.
* **Citation:** *The Impact of the Red Sea Crisis on Global Shipping and Strategic Supply Chain Management* ([Link](https://www.irejournals.com/formatedpaper/1711267.pdf))

## 3. Quantitative Risk Scoring for Import-Dependent Economies
* **Current Gap:** Geopolitical risks propagate through shipping networks via freight rate spikes and node disruptions, but existing platforms lack automated multi-agent risk quantification (MDPI).
* **ResilientIndia AI Solution:** Uses Geopolitical Scout Agents to convert qualitative intelligence into normalized threat scores (0–100) and automatically trigger mitigation workflows.
* **Citation:** *Geopolitical Risk and Shipping Supply Chain Resilience* ([Link](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-8954/14/4/427))
