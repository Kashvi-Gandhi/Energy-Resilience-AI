Paper 1:

The Research Gap: Recent research published in IJSFT / IJSRM highlights that traditional supply chain risk management approaches are predicated on historical data analysis and static contingency planning, making them completely inadequate for navigating dynamic, multi-dimensional geopolitical risks.

Your Solution: ResilientIndia AI replaces static contingency planning with a Gemini RAG Vector Engine + AI-powered Digital Twin Simulation. It ingests unstructured breaking news in real time, computes mathematical threat vectors, and simulates dynamic scenarios instantly.

Paper 2:

The Research Gap: Studies on recent maritime crises (such as the Red Sea / Bab-el-Mandeb disruptions and the Suez Canal blockages) show that when major chokepoints close, rerouting vessels around the Cape of Good Hope adds 10 to 14 days of transit delays, tripling freight rates and disrupting industrial supply chains. Because manual decision-making takes weeks, refiners suffer massive financial losses before taking action.

Your Solution: ResilientIndia AI collapses this response window from 47 days to under 3 seconds. The Logistics Architect Agent instantly calculates Cape of Good Hope bypasses, time delays (+10 days), and cost impacts the moment a threat score crosses safe thresholds.
